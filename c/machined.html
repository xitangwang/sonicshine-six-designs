Application notebook / Machined parts

Machining oil & trapped chips.

Machined housings and shafts can arrive with a film of cutting fluid and loose chips. Showing both the external surface and the deepest pocket helps define separate checks.

Workpiece illustration
Application briefBring a drawing or two photographs, a representative dirty part and the written limit that the next operation needs.

Photograph the part as it arrives

A single polished face may look clean while a threaded or blind feature still holds material. Mark the pockets, bores or clamping faces that need individual inspection.

Three questions for engineering

Material and finish
Is the substrate bare, coated or polished? Which faces must remain visually unchanged?
Residue
State whether the fluid is water-miscible or oil-based if known. Photograph typical and difficult deposits.
Target condition
Identify how the next operation will judge cleanliness: assembly, coating or visual inspection.

A candidate route for review

Consider a cleaning stage followed by a rinse and a check of the most difficult feature. If parts must leave dry, define how recesses will be checked before specifying a drying stage.
