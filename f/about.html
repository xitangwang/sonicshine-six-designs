About Sonic Shine

A project takes more than a specification.

Sonic Shine focuses here on industrial ultrasonic cleaning equipment and custom project discussions. A useful working brief joins procurement, production and site engineering.

Working approach

Give every question an owner.

Provide the workpiece information you already know. Use the first conversation to identify which details need a sample trial, a site review or a separate decision from your operations team.

Build a discussion agenda↗
Service / Before purchase

Agree the scope.

Discuss the incoming workpiece, the required exit condition and existing handling arrangements. A representative sample and a documented acceptance method help define a useful trial.

Read trial checklist · design PDF↗
Service / Installation and handover

Clarify the handoff.

Define who receives the equipment, which connections are by others, and what documentation will be reviewed. Installation, training and service scope must be agreed for the actual project.

Review handover questions ↗
Service / After delivery

Identify the equipment.

For an existing-equipment inquiry, include the equipment identifier, affected stage, observed issue and relevant photos when available. Confirm the appropriate support route for the installation.
