Automotive components.

Plan around mixed part geometry, batch handling and the next assembly operation.

01 / Production need

Describe what arrives.

Cast housings and other automotive components can present pockets that affect drainage, handling or inspection. Describe both the normal load and the difficult surfaces.

02 / Who brings what

Divide the questions.

Production lead
Provide the batch mix, normal loading arrangement and the step after cleaning.
Site engineer
Check whether the proposed transfer and access routes will fit the existing bay.
03 / Potential route

Discuss an equipment family.

A family is a starting point, not a final specification. The trial outcome, handling limits and site information can change the proposed arrangement.
