Company notes

How to read the equipment atlas.

Use the numbered callouts as a shared reference in a project discussion.

Locate the assembly

Begin with the main drawing. Each numbered marker refers to a design decision, not to a released part number or a fixed module. The tank, transfer, return loop and service side are shown together so their interfaces are visible.

Choose what needs review

Open a marker to see its role, the key question and the information needed from the site. Add the assembly to your focus list if it affects your project. A basket that drips during transfer, for example, may make both the transfer route and the tank interface relevant.

Carry the context forward

The inquiry button includes the selected assembly names in the project requirements field. Add your workpiece, residue and required finish; leave uncertain dimensions as questions. Final parameters belong in a reviewed proposal.
