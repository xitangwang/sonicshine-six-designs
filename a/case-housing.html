Illustrative process study / 01

Machined housing: planning a batch process.

A housing with intersecting passages and residual machining fluid.

Design example · no customer or performance claim

01 / Incoming condition

Start with the most difficult passage rather than the largest visible surface. Record where residue collects, how the housing was held during machining, and whether outlets allow fluid to drain.

02 / Proposed process review

Choose a basket orientation that exposes the problem area. Compare a dedicated wash stage with the need for a separate rinse before inspection.

03 / What to validate

Inspect the same locations before and after the trial. Record remaining residue, trapped liquid, part orientation and any handling difficulty.

No test result or production outcome is claimed in this example. An actual case would include the agreed test method, approved images and customer authorization.
