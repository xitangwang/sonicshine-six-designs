01 / Selection rationale

Where this family fits.

Use a dedicated cleaning stage when parts can be loaded as a batch and subsequent operations can be handled separately.

A repeatable group of parts with a defined basket and a separate downstream process.

Discuss these decisions

  1. Define the basket or fixture.

    Basket loading and unloading; lifting assistance reviewed against the loaded weight.

  2. Review the wash stage.

    Clean in a dedicated bath; agree any downstream rinsing or drying separately.

  3. Agree the handoff.

    Largest part and basket, loaded weight, contaminants and available floor space.
