What to include in a cleaning system brief.

A practical checklist for the first technical conversation.

Describe the workpiece

Start with the largest and most difficult part. Share its material, finish, dimensions and loaded basket weight. A simple sketch or photograph can explain access to recesses better than an overall dimension alone.

Identify the incoming condition

Describe what must be removed and where it collects. Distinguish loose particles from machining fluids or persistent films. Mention any process or chemical restrictions already in place.

Define the handoff

Explain what happens after cleaning: inspection, coating, assembly or storage. State the acceptance method if one already exists, and tell the equipment team how parts arrive and leave the process.

Prepare for a layout discussion

Include the loading direction, available space, access routes and utilities. If an input is uncertain, record it as a question rather than estimating it as a fixed requirement.
