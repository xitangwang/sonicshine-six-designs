Industrial equipment / Linked cleaning lines

Keep the checks linked.

A linked process can put washing, rinsing and drying in a defined sequence. The review must also address the handoffs between these stages.

Reference image Design illustration

Equipment note

Configuration guidance. Equipment size, process settings and production performance require an engineering review.

Follow the part from wet to dry.

Draw the route of one basket through the line, including any hold or drain points. Note where liquid can be carried into the next tank or retained at the exit.

What to record.

Proposed sequence
State the purpose of every stage; distinguish a cleaning requirement from a handling convenience.
Transfer and output
Give the batch size, loaded weight, target production pace and any pause between operations.
Exit condition
Identify the critical inspection area and the condition needed before assembly, coating or storage.
Plant interfaces
Mark floor space, loading points, utility availability and maintenance access for review.

Where the rinse ends

An agreed inspection point makes a rinse or drying issue easier to discuss. Define the time between leaving the machine and the observation, as well as the surface itself.
