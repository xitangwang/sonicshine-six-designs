Workpiece atlas / Machined components

Look into the feature.

Machined housings and manifolds can bring oil on the exterior and chips in a recessed feature. Treat those as separate inspection objectives.

Reference image Design illustration

Application note

Bring a marked drawing plus the actual acceptance question. The process should be reviewed against that question before a machine is specified.

Name the feature, then name the evidence.

Mark one exposed face and one difficult internal feature on a drawing. The outer face may be easy to photograph; the internal feature may need a different observation or sampling method.

Inspection brief.

Geometry
Show blind bores, cross-drillings, threads and any feature that may trap liquid.
Residue
Record whether the incoming workpiece carries an oil film, loose chips or an uncertain mixture.
Inspection
Agree what can be seen, what cannot, and which additional inspection is required.
Production
State when the component was last machined and how it moves to the next process.
