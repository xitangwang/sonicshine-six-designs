Field note / Acceptance records

Oil films and the reference patch.

Plan the observation before taking the photograph. A reliable visual comparison starts with the same patch of the same kind of part.

Surface contours / illustrative

Bench note

Use this guide to prepare the questions for your engineer or quality team. It is not a cleaning recipe or a validated inspection standard.

Identify the surface.

Mark a fixed patch on a drawing or photograph. Avoid comparing an exposed face on one part with a recess on another. Record whether the surface is machined, coated or polished.

Keep the viewing conditions consistent.

Agree the lighting direction and camera angle. Record whether the part is wet or dry and how long it has been held before observation. Surface appearance can change when any of these conditions change.

Agree what a photograph can resolve.

Describe the visual reference and what would trigger further investigation. If a downstream operation has a residue limit, agree a suitable measurement method with the quality team. An image alone does not establish that limit.

Keep the images with the brief.

Use a part identifier, feature label and observation date. Link the before and after records to the same criterion, so any engineering discussion can refer back to the original question.
