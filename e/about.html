Service / Project path

From first sample to proposal.

01
Define the incoming part

Bring a drawing, material, finish, residue and the condition required by the next operation.

02
Agree a trial record

Identify the observation area and method. Confirm sample handling and receiving arrangements before shipment.

03
Review a representative sample

Compare the recorded observations with the agreed criteria. Keep unknowns visible rather than treating them as passed checks.

04
Translate the record into a proposal

Review basket loads, transfer, utilities and production targets. Agree the service and documentation scope for the proposed system.
