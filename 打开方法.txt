Sonic Shine | Six industrial website concepts · Offline review

Extract the entire archive before opening it in a browser. Keep its folder structure intact.
Open index.html for the default English review, zh/index.html for Simplified Chinese,
or en/index.html for the preserved English entry point.

The language menu lists nine languages. English and Simplified Chinese are available.
Spanish, Italian, French, Russian, Arabic, Portuguese and Indonesian are reserved and have no translated pages.
The site contains 114 pages per available language plus three review entry points (231 HTML files).
The design guide is in shared/SonicShine-Six-Designs.pdf; its web buttons point to the online site.
Project enquiries are local drafts and are not sent automatically.
